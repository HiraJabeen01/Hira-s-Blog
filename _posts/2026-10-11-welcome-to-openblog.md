Welcome to **OpenBlog**! If you are reading this article, congratulations — your new blog template is officially live.

OpenBlog was crafted for creators, engineers, and writers who want a stunning, modern publication without the friction of databases, server management, or complex build setups.

## Why OpenBlog?

Most modern blogging platforms force you to choose between two extremes:
1. **Bloated CMS platforms** with endless plugins, monthly hosting fees, and security updates.
2. **Complex developer frameworks** that require Node.js, npm packages, and CI/CD pipelines to write a simple post.

OpenBlog strikes the ideal balance. It runs natively on **GitHub Pages**, meaning:
- **Free hosting forever** on GitHub's global CDN.
- **Zero build steps** for you — GitHub handles publishing automatically.
- **Write in Markdown** from anywhere, even directly inside the GitHub browser interface.
- **Total ownership** of your content and code.

## The 3-Step Setup

If you just cloned or generated this repository from the template, here are the only three things you need to do:

1. **Personalize `_config.yml`**:
   Open `_config.yml` and change the `title`, `author`, `bio`, and `social` handles to match yours.
2. **Enable GitHub Pages**:
   In your GitHub repository, navigate to **Settings** &rarr; **Pages**, set **Source** to **GitHub Actions**, and you're all set!
3. **Publish your first article**:
   Add a new `.md` file to the `_posts/` folder with today's date (e.g., `2026-10-12-my-first-post.md`).

## Designed for Aesthetics and Readability

Inspired by modern design systems like **shadcn/ui**, OpenBlog features:
- **Automatic Dark & Light Mode**: Seamless transitions with zero screen flash.
- **Custom Accent Colors**: Choose from Indigo, Blue, Emerald, Violet, Rose, or Amber in your config.
- **Rich Typography**: Optimized reading experience with Inter and JetBrains Mono.
- **Mobile Responsive**: Looks crisp on phones, tablets, and wide desktop screens.
